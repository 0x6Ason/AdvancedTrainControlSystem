# Code of conduct

ATCS is a small project about running model railways well, and the people around it should
have a good time. The rules are short.

- Be kind and assume good faith. Most disagreements are about what a railway needs, and there
  is usually a `network.yml` option that settles them.
- Judge the work, not the person. Reviews say what would make the change better and why.
- Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind, in issues, pull requests,
  discussions or elsewhere about the project, are not accepted and lead to removal.
- Keep other people's servers and players out of it: no coordinates, player names, IP addresses
  or private logs in public issues unless they are yours to share.

Something wrong? Contact the maintainer, [@0x6Ason](https://github.com/0x6Ason), without the
details in public, and you will be given a private way to send them. Reports are read by one
person and kept private.
